Lumen A1 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile Google shares (email and name). We use Google sign-in only to log you in; we do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or anything else in your Google account.
  • Username: the username you choose. It is public to other users of the app.
  • Study data: your course progress, words, review answers, mistakes, test scores and class tasks you save.
  • Community chat: the messages you post, shown with your username. Other users never see your email address.

What we do not collect

The app does not record audio, and does not use your location or contacts. Photos of exercises are read on your phone and are not uploaded.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, save your progress, and show your messages in the chat. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Who processes it

Your data is stored with our hosting providers (Supabase and Render). Text you send to the sentence checker or explanation features is sent, with phone numbers, emails and links removed, to AI and grammar-checking services (Google Gemini, Groq, LanguageTool) to produce the result. Chat messages are not sent to them.

Your choices

You can delete your own chat messages in the app. To have your account and data deleted, contact us at the address below.
